Before you start

  • One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude: do Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, before Chapter 1 ends. See its guide below.
  • No mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. There are 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave slot.
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep cheats off on your trophy save. Cheats only unlock after the story anyway.
  • One playthrough is enough. Free roam stays open after the story, and everything for 100% completion is still reachable then.
  • Two trophies are still unconfirmed. Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin have no verified trigger yet at launch. Their guides say so honestly instead of inventing a method.

Roadmap to the platinum

Three stages, about 200 hours in total. The story takes 60 to 80 hours, the 100% checklist about 100 hours, and the final cleanup 10 to 20 hours.

Stage 1

Finish the story, and meditate in Chapter 1

Play Chapters 1 to 5. All story trophies pop on their own. While you play, scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study new animals with binoculars, so the 100% checklist is already half done. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before the chapter ends.

Stage 2

100% completion and 70 of 80 challenges

After the story, open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. The checklist is lenient, about 60% of true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 300 of 397 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges. The only parts that need everything are robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests.

Stage 3

Strangers, side lines and cleanup

Finish the 8 qualifying Strangers and Freaks strands, the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and any miscellaneous trophies left. When the last one pops, the platinum follows.

Missable trophies: 1, Positive Mental Attitude (potentially). Every other trophy is still earnable after the story in free roam.
