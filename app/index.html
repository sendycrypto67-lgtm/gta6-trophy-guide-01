Before you start

  • One trophy can be missed. Positive Mental Attitude: do Lucia’s balcony meditation at Jason’s house in Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, before Chapter 1 ends. See its guide below.
  • No mission replay. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter. There are 15 manual save slots and 1 autosave slot.
  • Cheats disable trophies and saving. Keep cheats off on your trophy save. Cheats only unlock after the story anyway.
  • One playthrough is enough. Free roam stays open after the story, and everything for 100% completion is still reachable then.
  • Two trophies are still unconfirmed. Bad Luck, I Guess and Ten Pin have no verified trigger at launch. Their guides admit that plainly instead of inventing a method.

Roadmap to the platinum

Three stages and roughly 200 hours all in. The story runs 60 to 80 hours, the 100% checklist around 100 hours, and the last cleanup 10 to 20 hours.

Stage 1

Finish the story, and meditate in Chapter 1

Play through Chapters 1 to 5. Every story trophy pops by itself. Along the way, scan new vehicles with the WAiNK app and study new animals through binoculars, so the 100% checklist is already half finished. Do the Chapter 1 balcony meditation before that chapter closes.

Stage 2

100% completion and 70 of 80 challenges

Once the story is done, open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. The checklist is forgiving, close to 60% of true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 300 of 397 stunts, 70 of 80 challenges. Only robberies, underwater discoveries, shooting ranges, drone races and Bret’s quests need everything.

Stage 3

Strangers, side lines and cleanup

Wrap up the 8 qualifying Strangers and Freaks strands, the final missions for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy and Tyrone, and whatever miscellaneous trophies remain. When the last one pops, the platinum follows.

Missable trophies: 1, Positive Mental Attitude (potentially). Every other trophy is still earnable after the story in free roam.
